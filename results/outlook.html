Model quality

Calibrated logistic regression, chosen from five candidates on earlier weeks and scored once on the historical benchmark: 2026-09-13 to the map cutoff, four weekly blocks, each fitted only on what was known before it. The feasibility probe had already seen this window; the untouched holdout after the map cutoff is below. Brier is the mean squared error of the probability (lower is better); skill = 1 − Brier / the reference's Brier. References: clim28, the region × hour rate of the last 28 days, recounted daily; recent7, the region's rate of new alerts over the last 7 days. Intervals: 1,000 resamples of whole Kyiv days.
